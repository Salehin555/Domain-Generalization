\caption{\textbf{The decision procedure.} When a model in service is less accurate on a new group of entities, (1) a calendar check made from dates alone decides whether estimates from the observed history can be trusted; if not, the model is only blended with seasonal naive. Otherwise (2) the model is improved without target data, by training longer and blending or combining it with seasonal and classical forecasts chosen on source validation (Q2), and (3) the residual benefit of retraining on the target group is estimated against the improved model, at the deployment budget (Q1). The group is retrained on only if the estimate $E$ is at least the retraining cost $c$. Candidate groupings can optionally be ordered first by an input screen (Section~\ref{sec:6.1}). All outcomes depend on the models, data, budget and metric tested.}
